# SEO opportunities and risks

- **Cannibalization:** multiple Columbia/Lexington trivia, Music Bingo, DJ, corporate, private-event, and venue-entertainment URLs target similar intent. Keep them documented and redirected only where the replacement intent is unambiguous; compare Search Console landing-page queries before further consolidation.
- **Thin/editorial pages:** exported guides and regional pages need fact-checked content migration. Do not publish generic filler merely to hold a URL.
- **Local SEO:** strengthen Columbia and Lexington area pages with verified venues, service coverage, photos, and internal links. Irmo/Chapin and West Columbia/Cayce claims require owner confirmation.
- **Metadata:** the shared layout now provides unique title/description inputs, canonical, Open Graph, and Twitter card metadata. Add per-page social imagery as the photo library grows.
- **Structured data:** Organization is authoritative on home; services emit Service; dated events emit Event from central data. Add FAQ/Article/BreadcrumbList only when visible matching content exists.
- **Internal links:** event detail → venue/event index, venue → event details, service overview → service/booking, and audience pathways → relevant destinations are implemented.
- **AI discovery:** `llms.txt` states factual services, schedule, key URLs, and non-affiliation language. Keep it synchronized with central facts during future schedule changes.
- **Measurement:** identify existing analytics IDs and consent requirements before launch; none were guessed or added.
