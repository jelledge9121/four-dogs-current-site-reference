## Platform

## Users

Local guests finding a confirmed public event, and couples, venues, businesses, organizations, and private clients hiring entertainment.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors find something to attend and confidently request entertainment. Success means useful attendance instructions and completed booking inquiries.

## Operating Context

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the existing event JSON, recurrence rules, shared venues, timezone/status logic, stable URLs, and request-rendered discovery routes. America/New_York governs scheduling. Keep Netlify Forms field names, honeypot, booking endpoints, redirects, players, gallery, reviews, and integrations. Never publish private weddings, unconfirmed events, staffing, invented pricing, or unverified addresses. Test a pull-request Deploy Preview before production merge. Recovery uses a revert commit through the normal Git deployment path.

## Brand Commitments

Locally owned, authentic, energetic, approachable, professionally managed entertainment across South Carolina. Primary markets: Lexington, Columbia, West Columbia, Cayce, Irmo, Chapin, Fountain Inn. Preserve the logo, navy #0C0F26, cyan #29C4DC, cream #F2F0E8, and exact tagline: Bring your people. We’ll bring the Doggone Good Time. Only approved photos and verified testimonials. No stock substitutions, generated event imagery, fake proof, distorted branding, distressed lettering, or subscriptions.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

- Confirmed facts drive every event surface.
- Attendance and hiring remain clear visitor tasks.
- Preserve working systems and source identity.
- Reduce the effort to understand, choose, and act.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical: contrast, keyboard controls, labeled forms, reduced motion, and usable small-phone layouts. Browser checks do not certify physical devices or assistive technology.
